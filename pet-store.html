3 tips for a happy pup

1. Sniff walks count. Let them lead with their nose for 10 minutes, it tires them out more than a jog.

2. Rotate the toys. Put half away for two weeks, then swap. Old toys feel brand new.

3. Train with treats. Short 5-minute sessions beat long ones. End on a win, always.
